# Security

Please do not report security problems in public issues.

Email **[support@betterone.top](mailto:support@betterone.top)** with "Security" in the subject, and include:

- the version (`betterone-cs --version`) and the system it runs on;
- what an attacker can do, and what they need first (network access, an account, a role);
- the steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.

We reply to confirm we have received it, keep you informed while we work on it, and credit you in the release notes if you wish.

Fixes are released as new versions in [Releases](https://github.com/betteronetop/console-server-public/releases).

---

# 安全问题

请不要在公开的 Issue 里报告安全问题。

请发邮件到 **[support@betterone.top](mailto:support@betterone.top)**，标题写明"Security"，并附上：

- 版本号（`betterone-cs --version` 的输出）和运行的系统；
- 攻击者能做什么，事先需要什么条件（能访问网络、有账号、什么角色）；
- 复现步骤或验证代码。

我们会回复确认收到，处理过程中保持联系；如果你愿意，修复发布时会在更新说明里致谢。

修复以新版本的形式发布在 [Releases](https://github.com/betteronetop/console-server-public/releases)。
